# Deloo — tell us your event, we'll supply the setup

*Deloo* (from Greek δηλόω, *dēloō*: "to make visible, to make known").
Event and media equipment rental that starts from the event, not the item.

**Status:** Pivot decided 8 Oct 2026. The earlier poster tool (brand-locked posters for volunteer-run
organisations) is archived in `docs/archive/poster-tool/`. Its code stays in the repo until the rental MVP
replaces it (see `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` Phase 0).
**Domain:** deloo.space. **Spec:** `PRD.md`. **Build order:** `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

## The problem

Every weekend, churches, event planners, conferences, weddings and campus events in Nigeria rent LED screens,
sound systems, projectors, cameras, lights and streaming kits. Today that runs on WhatsApp and phone calls:

- **Nobody knows what's free.** You call five vendors to find one LED screen for Saturday.
- **Nobody knows what they need.** People ask for "a mic and a speaker" for an outdoor crusade of 2,000 people,
  then the back rows can't hear.
- **Nobody trusts anybody.** Renters pay deposits on faith. Vendors hand ₦20m of gear to strangers. Returns
  end in arguments about who broke what.
- **Equipment runs out at peak.** December, Easter, wedding season and conventions sell out every vendor at once.

## What Deloo does

1. **Plan:** a few tap-to-answer questions about the event (type, indoor/outdoor, crowd size, livestream,
   power, budget) → a recommended setup in Good / Better / Best, with a plain reason for every item.
2. **Supply:** live availability pooled from many vendors, churches renting out idle weekday gear, and
   individual owners. If something isn't available, we say so and offer alternatives.
3. **Trust:** verified renters and vendors, deposits, photo checklists at pickup and return, a technician
   included with high-value gear, and a rescue guarantee if a vendor cancels.

## Who it's for

- **Renters (first):** church media teams and event planners, reached through our church network. Then
  conferences, weddings, campus events, corporate launches.
- **Supply:** equipment rental companies, churches with idle weekday gear, individual owners (videographers,
  DJs, sound engineers).
