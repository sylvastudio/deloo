Decisions waiting on you

  1. Booking timings. Vendor accepts within 2 hours (30 minutes for short notice); free cancellation until 72 hours before; deposit back within 24 hours of a clean return; vendor has 12 hours for the return checklist.
  2. Handover photos. The vendor or technician shoots them, the renter reviews and confirms. With no signal, both phones confirm with a 6-digit handover code.
  3. Tab bar. Keep the native Android/iOS bar already in the app instead of the floating pill from the plan.
  4. Technician. A switch at booking (forced on for LED walls and other top-tier gear), not a planning question.
  5. No emoji in the UI. Gear categories get proper Material/SF icons.
